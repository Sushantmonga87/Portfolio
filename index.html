Featured product

ShareBills · Shared expense app

Good company.
Clearer expenses.

ShareBills brings flatmates’ bills, rent, and group expenses together—so everyone can see what’s shared, settle up with fewer UPI transfers, and get back to living together.

  • Equal, custom, and flexible bill splits
  • Monthly rent tracking and reminders
  • Smart balances and UPI settlements
  • Shared-space chat and bill disputes
  • Join spaces by QR or invite code
  • Reports and expense insights
