ABOUT

Building practical software, not just websites.

I work across the full application stack — backend logic, frontend interfaces, database architecture, and the deployment work that makes all of it usable in production.

Most of my experience comes from building operational systems for real businesses: a school ERP with fee ledgers and quarter-based billing logic, admin dashboards, lead-management tools, and business/e-commerce websites. These are applications where the interface is only half the job — the data model and business rules underneath have to hold up.

I'm comfortable owning a feature end to end: schema and migrations, controllers and models, Blade views, AJAX-driven interactions, reports and exports, authentication, and deployment to a live server. I also work on existing PHP/Laravel codebases — extending them, fixing them, and understanding logic I didn't originally write.
